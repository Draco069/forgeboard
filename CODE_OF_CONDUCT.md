# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Forgeboard project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

This Code of Conduct is written in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), adapted to a small
volunteer-maintained project.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and accepting that constructive
  criticism can be hard to hear.
- Taking responsibility for your mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning
  from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the people using the software.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or other community
  activity.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — the issue tracker, pull
requests and reviews, discussions, and any shared chat or forum used for the project —
and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public
spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to
the project maintainers, who are the owners of this repository on the hosting platform.

Use the platform's private channel so the report is not public:

- On GitHub, use the repository owner's profile to send a private message, or open a
  private report through the platform's abuse-reporting flow.

> **Maintainer note:** Forgeboard does not yet publish a dedicated conduct contact
> address. Add a monitored address here before announcing the project publicly. Until
> then, a direct private message to the repository owner is the reporting channel.

Please do not file a public issue or pull request to report unacceptable behavior, and
please do not include screenshots, logs, or personal details of other people in a
report sent through a channel others can see.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers will
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Corrective actions may include, in escalating order:

1. A private written warning, with clarity about the nature of the violation and why the
   behavior was inappropriate.
2. A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including a period of no
   interaction with the people involved.
3. A temporary or permanent ban from any sort of public interaction with the community.
4. A permanent ban from any public interaction with the community.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the other maintainers.

## Scope of responsibilities

Community members are expected to help maintain a positive, respectful environment.
They are not expected to manage the behavior of other community members outside the
project's spaces, and maintainers are not expected to act as moderators of conduct in
unrelated contexts.
